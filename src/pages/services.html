Pricing

Why there is no price list on this page

Because an honest number depends on things we cannot know from a web page: how many people, how many servers, what condition the environment is in, and what has to be fixed before it can be supported at all.

What we can promise about the shape of it: one flat monthly rate, quoted per user, for a scope written into the agreement. In the base rate: monitoring, patching, help desk, security, backup. Quoted separately: projects — migrations, AI automation and workflows, new-office builds — scoped and priced before work starts.

The number is the number, for the scope in the agreement. Anything beyond it is quoted before we start, never billed after. No annual escalator you find out about at renewal.

Twenty minutes on a call is enough for a real range, and the assessment that follows is free whether or not you hire us.
