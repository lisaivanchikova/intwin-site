Two ways to work with us

Most common

Fully Managed IT

You have no internal IT, or one person carrying far too much. We take day-to-day operations end to end and you set business priorities.

  • Monitoring, patching and maintenance
  • User support and onboarding
  • Hardware and licence procurement
  • Vendor wrangling — ISPs, line-of-business apps
  • vCIO planning and budget

Co-Managed IT

You have an IT lead or a small team. They keep the wheel; we add capacity, out-of-hours cover and specialist skills, with a written split of duties.

  • Overflow on tickets and projects
  • Nights, weekends and holiday cover
  • Security and cloud specialists on call
  • Tooling your team gets to use too
  • No turf war — the RACI is agreed up front

The services in detail

Fully Managed IT

Everything, one rate

Twenty-four-hour monitoring of servers, endpoints and network, with patching on a schedule you approve rather than whenever a vendor pushes. Asset and licence tracking so you stop paying for seats belonging to people who left. Procurement handled at our pricing. A named engineer who knows your environment, and a vCIO session each quarter covering what broke, what it cost and what to spend on next.

Co-Managed IT

Beside your own team

For internal IT that is stretched thin: a backlog that never clears, projects stalled behind daily tickets, and one person who cannot go on holiday. We take defined categories off the queue, cover the hours your team should not be working, and bring in specialists for the things a generalist should not have to learn under pressure. Duties are written down before we start, so nobody is guessing who owns what at 6pm.

Cybersecurity

Included, not an upsell

Managed endpoint detection and response, identity protection across Microsoft 365, email security filtering ahead of delivery, and a 24/7 security operations centre for what gets through. Multi-factor authentication rolled out in phases so adoption actually sticks. Security awareness training that runs on a schedule and produces a report you can hand to an insurer. When a cyber-insurance questionnaire arrives, we answer it with evidence instead of guesses.

Cloud & Microsoft 365

Migrate, secure, run

Microsoft 365 and SharePoint set up so permissions make sense and files are where people look for them. Migration from on-premises servers or from another provider, planned around your working week rather than ours. Licence right-sizing — most companies we assess are on the wrong plan in at least one direction. Remaining servers and virtual infrastructure monitored and maintained on the same agreement.

Backup & Business Continuity

Tested, not assumed

Backup for servers, endpoints and Microsoft 365 — the last of which most companies wrongly assume Microsoft is doing for them. Retention set to your actual obligations. The part that matters: we perform test restores on a schedule and send you the result, because a backup nobody has restored is a hypothesis. Documented recovery runbooks for the scenarios worth planning for.

Help Desk

A person, first time

Call during support hours and an engineer answers — no menu, no ticket-first rule, no callback promise. Email and portal for anything that is not urgent. Routine requests such as resets and provisioning often complete immediately through automation, which is precisely what frees the humans to answer the phone. Every interaction lands in your documentation, so the third person to touch your account knows what the first two did.

Pricing

Why there is no price list on this page

Because an honest number depends on things we cannot know from a web page: how many people, how many servers, what condition the environment is in, and what has to be fixed before it can be supported at all.

What we can promise about the shape of it: a flat monthly rate, quoted per user, with security and backup inside the number rather than bolted on. No hourly billing after an incident. No annual escalator you find out about at renewal.

Twenty minutes on a call is enough for a real range, and the assessment that follows is free whether or not you hire us.
