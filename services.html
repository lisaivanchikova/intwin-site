Two ways to work with us

Most common

Fully Managed IT

You have no internal IT, or one person carrying far too much. We take day-to-day operations end to end and you set business priorities.

  • Monitoring, patching and maintenance
  • User support and onboarding
  • Hardware and licence procurement
  • Vendor wrangling: ISPs, line-of-business apps
  • vCIO planning and budget

Co-Managed IT

You have an IT lead or a small team. They keep the wheel; we add capacity, out-of-hours cover and specialist skills, with a written split of duties.

  • Overflow on tickets and projects
  • Nights, weekends and holiday cover
  • Security and cloud specialists on call
  • Tooling your team gets to use too
  • No turf war, because the RACI is agreed up front

Pricing

Why there is no price list on this page

Because an honest number depends on things we cannot know from a web page: how many people, how many servers, what condition the environment is in, and what has to be fixed before it can be supported at all.

What we can promise about the shape of it: a flat monthly rate, quoted per user, with security and backup inside the number rather than bolted on. No hourly billing after an incident. No annual escalator you find out about at renewal.

Twenty minutes on a call is enough for a real range, and the assessment that follows is free whether or not you hire us.

What moves the number

  • Headcount and how fast it is growing
  • Servers, or none at all if you are fully cloud
  • Compliance obligations: SOC 2, HIPAA, insurer requirements
  • Condition on arrival: unsupported operating systems, no backup, no MFA
  • Whether you want us fully managed or beside an internal team
